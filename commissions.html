HOW IT WORKS

  1. 1.

    Briefing

    You send references, notes, or a written brief. I ask questions to lock down scope, deadline, and usage before any work starts.

  2. 2.

    Sketches

    Once the project and first invoice are approved, I get started on the initial sketch — pose, composition, and key details. You'll see this stage before anything is rendered, so it's still easy to adjust.

  3. 3.

    Refinement

    Once the sketch is approved and the second invoice is paid, I move into lining, coloring, and shading — bringing the piece to its finished look.

  4. 4.

    Delivery

    You'll receive the final files in the agreed format and resolution, plus any extras we discussed upfront — and that wraps up the project.

TERMS

Payment
50/50 or 100% upfront — your choice. If canceled after the first half is paid, 50% of that payment is refunded. If canceled after full payment, no refund.
Payment methods
Pix (Brazil). PayPal or Wise for international clients.
Revisions
Illustrations include up to 10 rounds. Character Design and Concept Art don't have a fixed limit — these are built from scratch and naturally involve more back-and-forth.
Usage rights
Personal use is included by default. Commercial, publishing, or merchandising rights are quoted separately and transfer only after full payment.
Turnaround
No fixed deadline for Character Design or Concept Art. For Illustrations: Flat Colors in 7 days, Full Render in 10 days.
Portfolio use
I may use finished commissions for my own portfolio and promotion. Let me know if you'd like your piece kept private.
Working files
Final files are delivered as flattened images. Layered PSD files are included on request at no extra cost.
What I don't take on
18+/explicit content, gore, and political content.
